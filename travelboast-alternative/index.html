Looking for a TravelBoast alternative?

If you want to turn a trip you already took into a short video, Tripmaxx offers a different starting point: your iPhone photo library. It finds trips from saved location details and places a moving route above your real photos and clips.

Two different ways to make a travel map video

TravelBoast is known for animated route videos. Its store listing describes a free export with a watermark and paid options that remove it. Tripmaxx takes a camera-roll-first approach: it finds a trip in photos with location data, then builds one vertical reel with the route on top and your own images below.

Tripmaxx is not a manual video editor or trip planner. It is designed for a retrospective recap from an iPhone photo library.

How Tripmaxx works

  1. It reads saved dates and location details to find trips in your Photos library.
  2. You choose a trip and tap once.
  3. Tripmaxx assembles a 9:16 reel on the iPhone: animated map above, real photos and clips below.
  4. Preview, export in Full HD, and share through the system share flow if you choose.

If no trip appears, use the included sample trip to preview the complete flow. The sample action is available on the Trip Discovery screen.

Privacy and requirements

Trip discovery and reel creation happen on device. Tripmaxx has no account, ads, tracking or developer backend for this flow. Map tiles and place-name lookups use network services. Photos remain in your library unless you choose to share an export. Tripmaxx requires iPhone with iOS 18 or later and location details saved with the photos.

Is Tripmaxx right for you?

It can fit if you have a multi-stop trip in your camera roll and want a quick shareable recap. It may not find a route if camera location was disabled, your pictures came from a camera without GPS, or you stayed in one place. It is not for Android or for frame-by-frame editing.

FAQ

Is Tripmaxx the same as TravelBoast?

No. The workflows differ. Tripmaxx builds a recap from trip photos and clips already in an iPhone library. TravelBoast focuses on animated route videos.

Do I need to record my route while travelling?

Tripmaxx looks for trip locations saved in iPhone photos, so it is designed for a recap after the trip. It is not a live trip tracker.

Does Tripmaxx upload my photos?

The on-device flow creates the reel on the iPhone. Photos stay in the library unless you choose to share the export. Map tiles and place-name lookups use network services.

What if Tripmaxx finds no trip?

Use the sample trip from Trip Discovery to preview the flow. For your own reel, the photos need saved location details and stops in more than one place.

Is Tripmaxx free to export?

Creating and exporting a reel on the iPhone is free.
