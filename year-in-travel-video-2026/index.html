Your year of travel, in one map reel

What makes a travel recap different?

A photo montage can show the moments. A map recap can also show where one trip ended and another began. Tripmaxx uses location details in iPhone photos to find trips and places their routes on the map above the footage. Year Recap is planned to bring that year's routes into the same format.

What Year Recap is planned to include

  • The routes from detected trips in 2026, brought together on one map.
  • Real photos and clips from those trips below the map.
  • A vertical 9:16 video assembled on device.
  • Full HD export in the on-device path.

Year Recap is in development. Feature availability depends on the shipped build. No trip count or distance is promised; the result depends on what location-tagged media exists in the library.

Get ready to make a recap

Tripmaxx needs an iPhone with iOS 18 or later and location data saved with photos. If location was disabled or all photos are from one place, there may be no route to recap. An included sample trip lets you explore the regular flow without importing personal photos.

Trip discovery and video creation happen on the iPhone. Map tiles and place-name lookups use network services. Photos stay in the library unless the user chooses to share the finished export.

A format for a personal year, not a leaderboard

Tripmaxx is a personal trip reel, not a social network or a travel diary. The year recap is meant to give the routes and moments a single visual frame. It does not rank travellers or compare trips.

The wider year-end period includes other recap products. Google Photos Recap has a short-video format but no travel map, and Strava's Year in Sport was placed behind a subscription in 2025. Tripmaxx focuses on a map of trips from the camera roll. These are different product choices, not like-for-like substitutes.

FAQ

Is Year Recap available now?

It is in development for December 2026. Check the current App Store listing for availability before planning around it.

What does the Year Recap show?

The planned format combines routes from 2026 trips on a map above the user's real photos and clips.

Does it need a travel-tracking account?

No account is required for Tripmaxx's on-device flow. It finds trips from location data in the iPhone photo library.

What if a trip has no location data?

Tripmaxx may not find that trip automatically. Photos from at least two locations are needed to draw a route; a sample trip can demonstrate the flow.

Are photos uploaded to make the recap?

For the on-device flow, the reel is created on the iPhone and photos stay in the library unless shared by the user. Map tiles and place-name lookups use network services.
